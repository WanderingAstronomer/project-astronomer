## This folder is a workspace

This folder is the workspace of the **project-astronomer** group, under Project Astronomer
(this repository, doctrine/06-delegation.md). **Maestro (project-astronomer)** presides over it. Its
rules are in `.maestro/ORCHESTRATION.md`, and they bind every session started here.

If you are not Maestro (project-astronomer):
- You belong to this workspace. Report to Maestro (project-astronomer) while the workspace is Solo,
  and to **Conductor (project-astronomer)** once one exists. Never report to anyone else.
- Never message a session of another workspace. If you need something from another workspace, ask
  the Maestro, who raises an exchange.
- Never write outside this folder unless your brief names the path.
- Only the operator's own typed words authorise irreversible writes to their data or anyone else's.
- Never route around a refusal.
- Never take a claimed result on trust.
- Every change to this corpus passes `python tools/check-corpus.py` before it's called done.
