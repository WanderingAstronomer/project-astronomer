# Unresolved

> **Doc status:** living.

## When

You went looking for a cause and could not establish one. The evidence is absent, contradictory,
or beyond what you can measure with what you have.

Recognizable by the pull toward a neighbouring state: you are about to file it as `UNVERIFIED`
because that sounds provisional and harmless, or as `REFUTED` because the leading hypothesis
failed, or to leave it out entirely because it is embarrassing.

**`UNRESOLVED` is a real result and the honest terminal state.** In astronomy, unresolved means
the instrument reached its limit and the observation reported that limit rather than guessing at
structure. It is the tail that vanishes from records that lack a name for it.

## Do

1. **Mark it `UNRESOLVED`** — in the field position, from the fixed vocabulary
   ([`doctrine/02-epistemics.md`](../doctrine/02-epistemics.md)). Not "unclear," not "probably,"
   not a hedge in the prose. A reader skims past a hedge; they cannot skim past a label.
2. **Distinguish it from its neighbours, explicitly.** `UNVERIFIED` means not yet checked.
   `REFUTED` means actively disproven. `UNRESOLVED` means **tried, and could not.** Rounding to a
   neighbour loses the fact that you spent the effort, and the next person will spend it again.
3. **State exactly what would settle it.** One falsifiable sentence: the observation that would
   decide the question. If you cannot write it, the problem is not the evidence — the question is
   not yet well formed, and that is what you record instead.
4. **State what instrument you would need**, and whether you have it. The measurement must be able
   to fail in the way that matters (L-12): what would have to go wrong for this to report a
   failure; is that the thing being claimed; if not, what is, and can it be measured here at all.
5. **Record the debt if it cannot be measured here.** "Owed to a better instrument" is an expected
   and acceptable outcome, and it is only useful written down.
6. **Park it in bucket E** — seen, attempted, not resolvable now — with the reason attached
   ([triage-pass](triage-pass.md)). Bucket E is a real disposition, not a bin. Parked *with a
   reason* is what stops it re-entering the queue at every review.
7. **Keep it.** Do not delete an unresolved item for tidiness. If it recurs in a year it will
   already have a first sighting, a date, and a record of what you tried.
8. **Do not act from it.** An unresolved cause is not a weak confirmed cause. Nothing is built on
   it until it moves.

## Record

- `TRIAGE BOARD` — bucket E, with the parking reason and the settling condition on the item.
- `FINDINGS` (frozen) — the attempt: what you looked at, what you found, why it was insufficient.
  A recorded dead end is worth more than an unrecorded one and costs the same.
- `DECISIONS` — only if parking it is itself a call someone might reverse: state what would reopen
  it under `blocks-on:`.

Exit condition: a stranger reading the item knows what was tried, why it stopped, and what would
restart it.
